## 2. APP DESCRIPTION & PURPOSE

**Primary Category:** Healthcare / Telehealth / Mental Health

**What the App Does:**
Lavni is a telehealth platform for therapy sessions between clients and therapists. It provides:
- Video call sessions with licensed therapists
- Real-time messaging with therapists
- Appointment scheduling and management
- Progress tracking for therapy sessions
- Insurance information and management
- Payment processing for therapy services
- Profile management and therapist matching

**Target Use Case:** Mental health therapy, counseling, and telehealth consultations
